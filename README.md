# Aetherbound

— A web-based, text-driven RPG and long-term endeavor, where you delve into dungeons, craft, duel, forge alliances, and shape your fate with magic. The game rewards consistency and commitment, much like the real world, and needs but five minutes a day to stay ahead of your adversaries. Make your name known.

---

## Features

### Dungeon Exploration
- Descend into treacherous dungeons with skill checks, puzzles, monsters, and bosses.

### Combat & Magic
- Initiate combat using Aether, use magical abilities, and resolve encounters based on your stats.

### Player-Driven Economy
- Jobs, crafting, and resource management affect the world.

### Guilds & Co-op
- Form guilds, run dungeons together, and engage in guild wars.

### PvP Integration
- Optional duels and PvP encounters, with Honor and Infamy shaping outcomes.

### Dynamic Stats & Skill Checks
- Level up mental stats (Intelligence, Perception, Charisma, Stealth, Willpower) to succeed in actions.

### Resource Management
- Health, Aether, Energy, and Resolve bars with boosters and cooldowns to manage risk.

### Interactive World Map
- Hoverable icons for NPCs, markets, and dungeons.

## Tech Stack

- **Backend:** Laravel (PHP)  
- **Frontend:** Blade Templates + Tailwind CSS + Alpine.js  
- **Database:** MySQL
